# gastos privacy policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

gastos is a personal finance tracker. It is built to keep your money data private.

## What gastos stores

- **On your iPhone:** everything you enter, including wallets, balances, transactions, categories, budgets and recurring items. This data is stored in the app on your device and is protected by iOS. It stays there even if you never create an account.
- **In your gastos account (optional):** if you choose **Sign in with Apple** in Settings, the same data is also stored on our server so it can sync between your devices. We also store:
  - your Apple account identifier
  - the email address Apple shares with us, which can be a private relay address if you pick "Hide My Email".

We don't collect anything else. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, and no access to your contacts, location, photos or bank logins. gastos never connects to your bank.

## How it's stored and protected

- Synced data is stored by our database provider, [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), in Singapore (region `ap-southeast-1`).
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
- Each account can only read and change its own records. The database enforces this with row-level security.
- **App lock:** Face ID, Touch ID and passcode unlock are handled entirely by iOS. gastos never sees your biometric data.

## How we use it

Only to run the app: showing your data and syncing it between your devices. We don't sell, rent or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising or profiling.

## Your choices

- **Use gastos without an account.** Nothing leaves your iPhone.
- **Sign out** in Settings. Syncing stops, and data on your iPhone stays.
- **Delete your account** in Settings → Sync → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything stored on our server. Data on your iPhone stays until you delete the app.

## Children

gastos isn't directed at children under 13.
